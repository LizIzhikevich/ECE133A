ECE 133A: Applied Numerical Computing

UCLA, Fall 2026

ECE 133A introduces the methods and tools of numerical computing. We will study how computers represent and approximate mathematical problems, with particular attention to accuracy, stability, efficiency, and practical implementation.

🚩 Course Structure

Final course grades are based on the following three components:

📝 Quizzes (70%)

The first discussion has no quiz. Subsequent non-holiday discussions include a quiz covering the prior week’s Tuesday/Thursday lecture material. Your two lowest quiz scores will be dropped.

💬 In-person discussion activities (20%)

Discussion activities are completed in person during Friday discussion. Up to two activity scores may be dropped.

🔬 Final project (10%)

Students will complete a final project applying numerical-computing methods to an engineering problem. Project requirements and milestones will be announced during the quarter.

⚙️ Administrivia

All submitted work must be original work. Please speak with the instructor before using generative AI tools for course work or the final project.
